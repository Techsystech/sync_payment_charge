Add Fees In Customer Payment

This module allows you to charge the fees onto your customers by adding them to the total amount.

Our module offers an open configuration option to set payment fees as a percentage, as well as a fixed amount, for both domestic and international cards.

Once the payment fees are configured, the system automatically calculates and adds them to the total amount.

Key Features
Fees Paid by Customer The system allows you to charge payment fees to your customers. You can configure these fees in the payment settings to calculate the total amount.
Payment Amount Calculation This feature enables precise computation of the total amount due, including any applicable charges or fees, ensuring that vendors receive the correct net payment amount after all deductions.
Auto Calculate Charge As per configuration, the system will automatically calculate charge/fees from total amount.
Payment The Payment Transaction displays both the payment amount and the associated fees.
